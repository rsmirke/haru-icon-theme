# Artwork

Finished squircle drawings used with `style = "art"` in the config.
Each one is Yaru's own artwork, taken from the original Yaru source files:
the icon's background is reshaped to fill the squircle and every pictogram
keeps its exact Yaru proportions. Made with `tools/make-art.py`.

Based on the Yaru icon theme (https://github.com/ubuntu/yaru) by the Ubuntu
community, licensed CC BY-SA 4.0. These derived drawings are under the same
licence.

`com.github.unrud.VideoDownloader.svg` is drawn from Video Downloader's own
icon (https://github.com/Unrud/video-downloader, GPL-3.0): its film strip,
gradients and arrow, laid out to fill the squircle.

The `org.libreoffice.LibreOffice.*.svg` drawings are made with
`tools/libreoffice-art.py` from Yaru's LibreOffice icons: the coloured page
fills the squircle with a folded corner, and the start centre's page is a
smaller squircle inside its grey.
