# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (17 November 2026)

First public release.

- Every application icon on one squircle shape (superellipse, n = 5, 87.5 % of
  the icon) with a hairline rim; folders, file types, symbolic and status icons
  stay exactly as Yaru draws them.
- 35 hand-made squircle drawings, most from Yaru's own artwork, among them Maps,
  Terminal, Text Editor, Backups, Evolution, Builder, Videos, Rhythmbox,
  Shotwell, Transmission and all seven LibreOffice apps.
- Round icons (Steam, Discord, Zoom, OBS Studio…) have their disc carried out
  to fill the squircle; other icons sit on a plate chosen to suit them.
- 99 icons reviewed and frozen: their look is pinned in `config.toml`
  (list in `docs/validated-icons.md`).
- Works with snaps, Flatpaks and AppImages; follows your accent colour and
  light or dark style; rebuilds itself when apps are installed or removed.
- Installs in your home folder, no sudo; `uninstall.sh` puts everything back.
